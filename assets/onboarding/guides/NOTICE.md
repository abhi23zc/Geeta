# Audited settings screenshots

Eligible manufacturer screenshots are reproduced from **Urbandroid Team / dontkillmyapp.com** under the compiled site's **CC-BY** license. [The project's license statement](https://github.com/urbandroid-team/dont-kill-my-app#licence) permits commercial sharing and adaptation with attribution. WebP conversion/resizing is an adaptation; original annotations remain. The app renders additional normalized control outlines without altering the source images.

`sources.json` records original source URLs/hashes, bundled dimensions, manufacturer/brand, software version (or explicitly unknown), setting/UI variant, attribution, redistribution terms, eligibility and highlight coordinates. In-app examples show attribution, source links and actual software labels. Example app names differ; users select geeta.

The notification dialog files are simulated app artwork, despite the previous source claim. They are **quarantined**, excluded from the screenshot renderer, and never presented as device captures. Generic Android channel/fullscreen images are retained for provenance but excluded from manufacturer guides because their manufacturer is unverified. The supplied attribution claims for those records have not been promoted into screenshot eligibility.

Huawei screenshots are not used for Honor; Samsung screenshots are not used on other brands. The OnePlus image demonstrates auto-launch, not battery optimization; Vivo background-power imagery is not an autostart example. Unknown software versions are disclosed.

`coverage.json` and `docs/onboarding-screenshot-coverage.md` list every applicable page and missing matching capture. No manufacturer currently has complete screenshot support. Permission-cleared device captures or appropriately licensed matching screenshots are required to close those dependencies. Missing images never omit pages or produce fabricated examples.

Project captures added 2026-10-10: Xiaomi M2101K7BI, MIUI 14 / Android 13, captured at the user’s request for Geeta offline guides. The six xiaomi-miui14-*.png files are unmodified device captures. Settings UI belongs to its respective owners. Native dimensions, hashes and highlights are in sources.json.
